# Donations

Optional donations support Track Anything development. They do not unlock
features, content, or access; the app remains fully free.

Open **Donations → Support on Patreon** in the app, or visit
[Track Anything on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/PearceMullins).

The link opens a separate tab. Patreon handles payments and account details.
Track Anything does not collect payment information or send tracker data there.
