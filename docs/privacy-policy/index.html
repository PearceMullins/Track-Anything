Privacy Policy

Track Anything — effective October 11, 2026

This notice describes the standalone HTML app, whether opened locally or from its hosted page.

Tracker data

Profiles, entries, notes, equations, saved lists, and interface preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. No account is required. The app has no advertising or analytics and does not upload tracker data to developer servers. Application styling and logic are included in the HTML file.

Export creates a backup file containing your tracker data and preferences. Import reads a file you select and replaces current app data after confirmation. Sharing uses your browser's sharing controls, when available, or downloads a backup. Data leaves your browser only when you choose to export or share it. Keep backup files private if they contain sensitive information.

Hosting and external links

Opening the hosted app requests the HTML from GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary connection information such as your IP address under its privacy statement. Your tracker entries are stored locally, separately for each browser and origin.

Donations opens Patreon in a separate tab. Patreon handles accounts, payments, and any information you provide there under its own privacy policy. The tracker does not collect payment information or send tracker data to Patreon.

Retention and deletion

Data remains until you delete it in the app, use Factory reset, or clear the browser's storage. Private browsing can discard it when closed. Moving a downloaded HTML file or switching browsers can change the storage available to the app. Export backups before making those changes.

Factory reset deletes the app's local profiles, entries, equations, and lists. It does not delete backup files you previously exported or data stored in another browser or copy of the app.

Security and contact

Local storage is not encrypted by the app. Your browser, device security, and backups affect its protection. Keep your browser and device updated. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Policy changes will appear here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions, open an issue in the repository, without posting private tracker data.
